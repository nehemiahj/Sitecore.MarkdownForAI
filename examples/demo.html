Chronic Kidney Disease in Cats

In short: chronic kidney disease (CKD) is a slow loss of kidney function that is common in older cats. Diet, hydration and regular check-ups help many cats live comfortably for years.

Share: Facebook · Email · Print

Signs to watch for

  • Drinking more than usual
  • Peeing more, with bigger clumps in the litter box
  • Weight loss and a dull coat

Emergency: go to a vet now if your cat may have touched any part of a lily or licked antifreeze.
